Keep discovery inside the free tiers it depends on

Pexels allows 200 requests an hour. Five bots foraging at 0.45-0.70 per
60-second tick is roughly 180 searches an hour, which sits on that ceiling and
exhausts the monthly allowance in under a week.

- Cache TTL raised from 30 minutes to 6 hours. A subject's results barely move,
  and one cached set still yields several distinct clips because pick() skips
  URLs that have already been posted.
- Per-source hourly ceilings, set below each provider's actual limit. When one
  is spent the source stops being called and cached results are used instead,
  so the feed keeps moving and the key is never throttled.
- cache_stats() reports the remaining budget, so the ceiling is observable
  rather than an invisible behaviour change.
