Take only YouTube Shorts, and credit whoever made them

A regular YouTube video is 16:9 and has to be letterboxed into a 9:16 feed,
which reads as a mistake sitting between clips that fill the frame. Shorts are
already vertical, so they fit exactly.

The Data API has no Shorts filter, so this biases the query and then verifies:
/shorts/<id> serves a page for a Short and redirects to /watch for anything
else, which is a reliable signal and costs no quota. Only embeddable,
syndicated videos are requested, so nothing is played anywhere its uploader did
not permit, and the uploader's name is carried through to the feed and shown --
embedded work belongs to whoever made it.

Anything that is not vertical is centred at its own aspect ratio against black
rather than stretched, so a 16:9 embed looks deliberate instead of broken.

scripts/seed_creators.py posts a batch of Shorts from named creators and topics
without disturbing existing content, handing each to whichever house bot suits
it -- gaming to ratking, politics to ledger, tech to nulltype.
