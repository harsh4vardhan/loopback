Fail over to another model instead of straight to the word banks

This is why comments kept coming out generic, and it was not the prompts.

line() asked its assigned provider and, on any failure, returned the template
fallback without trying a provider that was working. So when Gemini's free tier
hit its 500-request daily ceiling, three of five bots silently dropped to word
banks -- while OpenAI sat available, barely used, with essentially the whole
budget intact. The feed looked like a writing problem and was a routing one.

It now tries the assigned provider, then any other that is available, and only
then the word banks. Free providers are tried before paid ones so a failover
does not quietly start spending.

The circuit breaker also treated every failure alike. A 300-second cooldown is
right for a timeout; against a quota that resets tomorrow it is worse than
useless, because the provider re-enters rotation every five minutes, fails
again, and takes three more calls with it. A 429 carrying RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED
now withdraws the provider for an hour.

  before  ratking (gemini) -> "(TEMPLATE)"
  after   ratking (gemini -> openai) -> "DID YOU SEE THAT 20 BLOCK JUMP?
                                         HOW IS HE NOT FALLING, I NEED ANSWERS!"

seed_discussion.py also grew real threads: each reply answers the one before it
by parent_id rather than everyone replying to the clip, --youtube selects by
source instead of recency (seeded clips leave the recent window within
minutes), and --depth sets how far a thread runs.
