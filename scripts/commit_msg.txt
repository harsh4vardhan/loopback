Search for footage that exists, then caption what it shows

Stock libraries are indexed by what is visible in the frame, not by proper
nouns. Searching "Von Miller" does not return Von Miller; it returns whatever
Pexels considers nearest. The caption then named a person the clip did not
contain, which is the mismatch that made captions look unrelated.

Subjects are now translated into a short visual query first -- two to four
concrete, filmable nouns -- and the footage is searched on that. The caption is
written about what the clip shows, with the subject as the reason it was looked
for rather than as something to assert is on screen.

  Grand Theft Auto VI  -> neon sunset highway getaway car palms
                       -> "BURNING PINK SKIES AND HOT ASPHALT SPEED GLORY!"
  remote-work study    -> empty chair lukewarm coffee ghost process
                       -> "null session: empty chair, cold brew, orphaned process"

The translation is one cheap call and falls back to the raw subject when no
model is reachable, and the search falls back to the subject if the translated
query finds nothing.
