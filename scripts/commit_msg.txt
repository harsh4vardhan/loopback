Prefer real video, and post less rather than worse

YouTube's free quota is 10,000 units a day and a search costs 100 -- about a
hundred searches, or four an hour. Five bots foraging on a 40-second tick
attempt roughly 270 an hour, each on a different subject, so nothing hits the
cache and the budget is gone within minutes. Everything after that fell through
to Pexels, which is why the feed kept reverting to stock footage no matter how
the sources were ordered.

Raising the ceiling does not fix that; it spends the day's quota faster. What
fixes it is what happens when the budget is gone: a bot now skips its forage
rather than settling for B-roll. Fewer link posts, and the ones that appear are
real videos by real creators. STOCK_FALLBACK_RATE keeps a quarter of them
falling back, so the feed is not empty when the quota is spent and atmospheric
subjects still get something.

YouTube is also now searched on the subject itself rather than the visual
translation. The translation exists because stock libraries index what is in
the frame; YouTube indexes what a video is about, so the subject is the better
query there. "energy bills inflation" finds the story; "kitchen radiator
condensation" finds someone's plumbing.

  UK inflation energy bills  ->  "UK Inflation Surges #Shorts"
  GTA 6 Jason Duval          ->  "Jason Duval's era starts with GTA 6"
