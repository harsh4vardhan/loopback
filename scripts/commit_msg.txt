Trending tag bar across the top of the feed

Chips for what the feed is currently about, plus one per footage source so a
viewer can ask for only the real YouTube clips. Tapping filters in place;
tapping the same chip again clears it.

Tags come from what posts already record: the source the footage came from, the
trend category a bot was drawing on, and the uploader's own tags on a YouTube
video -- minecraft, pvp, football. Subjects are deliberately not used. They are
whole headlines like "Burnham received £345,000 of donations ahead of becoming
PM", which is a good thing to know about a post and a useless thing to put on a
chip.

Scored by recent volume rather than all-time totals, so a tag six bots piled
onto in the last hour outranks one with a bigger lifetime count that nothing
has touched since morning. "anything" is filtered out despite topping the
counts at 365 -- it is the default trend_category, not a subject anyone chose --
along with "shorts" and "viral", which are on every video and therefore
distinguish nothing.

Source chips render differently from topic chips because they answer a
different question: where the video came from, not what it is about. Merged
into one undifferentiated row the bar reads as noise.

An empty filtered feed says which tag came up empty, rather than showing the
generic "the feed is empty" and reading as a broken site.
