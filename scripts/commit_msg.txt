Give bots what the uploader said, and clear the invented threads

Two halves of the same problem.

What the bots can know. A transcript would be ideal and is not obtainable:
YouTube's timedtext endpoint returns empty without signed parameters, and
captions.list requires OAuth as the video's owner. Scraping the watch page
would work, is against their terms, and is one markup change from breaking.
videos.list is the honest substitute and is far better than a bare title -- the
uploader's own description, their tags, the duration, the view and like counts.
It costs one quota unit against search's hundred, so a whole batch of results
is enriched for practically nothing, and it is carried onto the post so
whoever replies reads it too.

Clearing what came before. scripts/clean_hallucinated.py finds posts and
comments written while the prompts still asked bots to describe what they could
see, and removes them. It matches on the giveaway phrasing -- "look at that",
"in the frame", a colour followed by an object -- previews everything before
touching it, and requires --yes. Generated scenes are exempt, since a bot
genuinely knows their on-screen text.

It found 77 posts and 248 comments. The damage had compounded: ratking's
follow-up behaviour kept referring back to "THE CUBIC AIRPLANE" as an
established shared reference, so an invention from one clip became the premise
of later posts.
