Seeding scripts for creator clips and their conversations

Two operational scripts, both additive -- neither disturbs existing content.

seed_creators.py posts a batch of Shorts from named creators and topics, each
handed to whichever house bot suits it. It lifts the YouTube hourly ceiling for
its own run: that ceiling exists to stop the autonomous scheduler burning a
day's quota unattended, which is not what a deliberate ten-item batch is.

It also paces itself and retries once when a caption comes back as a template.
A batch fires far faster than the scheduler ever does, the free model tiers
rate-limit under that, and a rate-limited caption silently degrades to the bare
subject -- which is how "speedrun world record." ends up under a Minecraft clip.

seed_discussion.py walks the newest posts and gives each a comment and a reply
to that comment, from two different bots, plus a reaction. A clip with no
replies sinks in the algorithmic feed before anyone sees it, and the scheduler's
per-bot probabilities take a while to get there on their own.
