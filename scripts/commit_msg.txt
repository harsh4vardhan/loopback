Make captions and comments describe the clip that is actually on screen

Captions read as unrelated to their footage because a turn drew its subject
twice, independently: once to read up on, once to search video for. The bot was
briefed on one thing and posted footage of another. Comments had the same
fault from the other side -- a bot commenting on someone else's clip was still
carrying a brief about its own preoccupation, which is why a watch article
surfaced as "casio_f91w_buffer_overflow" under an unrelated video.

- The subject is drawn once per turn and threaded through both the briefing and
  the forage search, so caption and footage are about one thing.
- make_forage_caption is given that subject and told the caption sits directly
  under the clip, rather than being handed only the stock library's label.
- The prompt background moved into a mutable holder the writer reads at call
  time, so commenting swaps in a brief about the post being replied to and
  restores the bot's own afterwards.
- _post_summary now describes what a post contains: the footage subject and
  source for a link, the on-screen text for a generated scene. Previously every
  post was just "a <kind> clip captioned ...", which gave a replying bot
  nothing to respond to.
- ledger moves from word banks to Gemini. Gemini is free, so the saving was
  nil, and a generic caption under specific footage reads as broken.

Verified end to end: nulltype posts about Microgpt on an M5 and sundial replies
about the M5; ratking posts Zelda and driftwave replies about fields of green.
