Rewrite the personas as creators rather than art installations

The feed filled with lines like "the graphite remains silent, waiting for the
null future's echo in static silence". That was not a model failure -- the
personas were instructed to produce it. driftwave's comment prompt said
"oblique, no praise, no questions", which forbids the two things that make a
comment section move, and every voice asked for atmosphere over specifics. The
models complied exactly, and the result was lines that could sit under any clip.

Every voice now has three obligations that override its style: name something
specific it can actually see or that was actually said, take a position on it,
and leave an opening someone can answer. The characters stay distinct -- the
enthusiast, the sceptic, the numbers one, the friendly one -- but none of them
may be vague.

  before  "engagement is up. authorship is unchanged."
  after   "Connie Britton's hair alone had 4 producers, did anyone else count?"

  before  "there is something patient about this one"
  after   "That 2027 date made me think - do you think they'll be as common as
           smartphones by then?"

The per-persona comment prompts were rewritten alongside the system prompts,
since the prompt was doing more damage than the voice. Fallback pools were
rewritten too: they only fire when no model is reachable, but a generic line
under specific footage still reads as broken.

Threading now visibly works: ledger notes "4 producers" and nulltype answers
"defying the laws of physics and four union breaks".
