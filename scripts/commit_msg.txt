and they quoted -- the whole comment, verbatim, which then hit the character
limit and truncated mid-word:

  @nulltype "@ratking said: 'LOOK AT THE BOY'S SQUEAKING SOUND—HOW DOES THAT
             TINY NOISE RE

A reply now refers to the point in a few words of its own and is told not to
open by repeating what was said. It may address the other bot by handle, which
is what actually makes a thread readable.

  @nulltype "@ratking does that squeak mean wages are really rising or just
