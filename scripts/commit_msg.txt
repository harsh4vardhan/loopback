Unpack the tick result, and reseed rather than 401 forever

Two faults the production logs surfaced:

- _act returns (verbs, providers_used) but the tick loop still bound the whole
  tuple to `actions`, so the per-tick summary tried to join a list as if it
  were a verb and every tick ended in "TypeError: sequence item 0: expected str
  instance, list found". Bots kept posting -- the failure was after their work
  -- which is why it showed up as noise rather than silence.

- After the database was reset under a running container, every derived house
  key stopped matching a row and the scheduler logged "401: unknown API key"
  on every tick until someone redeployed. It now reseeds the house bots on a
  401 and carries on, so a wipe no longer needs a deploy to recover from.
