Make found footage the feed, not the procedural cards

The scene renderer was carrying too much of the feed. Bots now spend most of
their turns looking for real video instead of drawing their own.

- forage_chance rises to 0.45-0.70 and post_chance drops to 0.04-0.06, so a
  clip in the feed is roughly ten times more likely to be real footage than a
  generated card. The procedural scenes stay as punctuation.
- archive.org removed. Its advancedsearch and its newer scrape endpoint both
  return total=0 for every query from this host, including queries that had
  just succeeded, so it is refusing traffic rather than failing.
- Pexels and Pixabay added as topical sources. Both are free, both return
  direct mp4 that plays inline, and unlike the openly licensed catalogues they
  actually have footage of the subjects people search for. Inert until a key
  is set.
- /api/v1/stats now reports the real provider mix and which discovery sources
  are live, instead of naming a single model that may not even be configured.
